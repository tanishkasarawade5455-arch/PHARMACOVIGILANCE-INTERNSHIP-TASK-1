# Pharmacovigilance Internship – Task 1

## ADR Identification Basics

### Objective

To analyze a patient case scenario, review symptoms, medication history and timeline, identify potential adverse drug reactions (ADRs), and determine the suspected drug.

### Patient Scenario

- Patient: 45-year-old female
- Symptoms: Rash, itching, nausea and dizziness
- Suspected drug: Amoxicillin
- Concomitant medication: Paracetamol as needed
- Amoxicillin started: Day -5
- Symptoms began: Day -2
- Symptoms worsened: Day 0

### ADR Identification

The reported symptoms were:

- Rash
- Itching
- Nausea
- Dizziness

Amoxicillin was identified as the suspected drug based primarily on the temporal relationship between drug initiation and symptom onset.

### Causality Considerations

The available information supports a possible relationship between amoxicillin and the reported symptoms. However, definitive causality cannot be established from the limited case information.

Dechallenge and rechallenge information were not provided.

### Conclusion

The case demonstrates the basic process of ADR identification by reviewing symptoms, medication history and treatment timeline. Amoxicillin is the suspected drug based on the available information.

### Key Pharmacovigilance Points

- Review the patient's symptoms and medication history.
- Establish the temporal relationship between drug exposure and event onset.
- Consider concomitant medications.
- Avoid making a definitive causality conclusion when information is incomplete.
